# hz-bright triage 1: the 14 stars that passed LEO-Vetter so far (2026-10-24)

**Result: 1 FULL-VET, 4 KNOWN, 9 FAIL.** The one worth a full vet is **TIC 149928372** (16.365 d, 903 ppm, 2.2 Re, 8.7 S_earth). It is a clean, single, U-ish dip with consistent depths. It is not a TOI, not a CTOI, not in Eumemic, and has no 2-minute SPOC TCE. But it *is* a TESS-SPOC FFI TCE from Sector 47, which ExoMiner++ 2.0 scored 0.037 (FP). The other two priority stars are already known. 293689266 is the 14.8 d CTOI 293689267.01, which Fraser (2026) places on a star 33" away. 402339417 is a published Eumemic tier-B candidate.

Numbers come from `triage1.csv`, made by `scripts/triage.py`. That script is hz-wide triage2's script with only the path, the star list and the output name changed (`diff` shows 3 lines). It ran on copies of the Spark files in `data/`: `res_v4_gpu/<tic>.json`, `lc/<tic>.npz`, every `v4/leo/cache/hz-bright_<tic>_gpu*.json`, and `v4/leo/candidates.csv`. The `iter` for each star is the one in `candidates.csv`. There is one fold plot per star at `png/TIC<tic>.png` (P, phase 0.5, 2P even/odd, full phase, masked periodogram) and per-epoch depths at `epochs_<tic>.csv`. Saved sources are in `_sources/`, each with a `.url` file. "LEO" values come from `data/hz-bright_<tic>_gpu<iter>.json` (`metrics`).

## Limits
- No pixel or centroid work was done. 149928372's full vet needs it, plus the S47 DV report.
- ExoMiner++'s 0.037 FP score for the 149928372 FFI TCE is unexplained here (*inference:* a single-sector MES 8.8 TCE at twice our depth may have been fit to a noisy subset).
- The FWHM and edge/centre columns are coarse at 30-minute binning for 1-1.5 h dips.
